# DenziDownloader Privacy Policy

The DenziDownloader Chrome extension does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It reads the URL of the tab you click it on and sends it only to the DenziDownloader desktop app running on your own computer (127.0.0.1). No data is sent to the developer or any third party by the extension. Settings (such as your chosen download folder) are stored locally by the desktop app.
